FAQ

Is the terminal really free?

Yes. The macOS app is open source and free forever. Local agents run with your own API keys at no cost to you. Cloud VMs are part of the paid plans.

What are Cloud VMs?

Persistent Linux machines in the cloud: pets, not cattle. Following the Zen of cmux, a machine is a primitive, not a managed solution: it keeps its files, installed tools, and state, and what you build on it is yours. Agents clone repositories, run commands, and open pull requests there without touching your Mac. Pro has no cap on how many Cloud VMs you keep: machines are metered beyond the included usage, and your spending cap is the stop. One VM can use up to 16 GB RAM / 8 vCPU; the 24 GB / 12 vCPU and 32 GB / 16 vCPU sizes require Max. Team is optional: it puts everyone on one shared bill and one spending cap.

What does Max add?

Max is $200/mo, or $1,920/yr ($160/mo) when billed yearly. It includes everything in Pro and the larger Cloud VM sizes: 24 GB / 12 vCPU, 32 GB / 16 vCPU, and up to 64 GB RAM / 32 vCPU on one VM. One Pro or Team VM can use up to 16 GB RAM / 8 vCPU. Pro, Team, and Max do not cap how many VMs you keep (Plus keeps one): machines are metered beyond the included usage, and the spending cap is the stop. Free accounts do not include Cloud VMs.

Do I still need Tailscale for the iOS app?

No. The iOS app pairs with your Mac over the built-in cmux iroh network. You can also bring your own networking, like Tailscale. Enterprise can self-host.

How does billing work?

Pro is $50/mo, or $480/yr ($40/mo) when billed yearly. Max is $200/mo, or $1,920/yr ($160/mo) when billed yearly. Team is $60/user/mo. The terminal and local agents stay free.

Can I self-host the cloud?

Yes, on Enterprise. Run agent execution on your own infrastructure or fully air-gapped, so source code never leaves your network.

Do you have a team plan?

Yes. Team is $60/user/mo with centralized billing for your whole team and priority support.
