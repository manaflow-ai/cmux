# 2026-11-26-mac-bun-r2

On the six `Restore pinned Bun 1.4.4 from the CI cache bucket` steps in `.github/workflows/cmux-next.yml`
(`env: { TOOLCHAIN_R2_ALL: "1" }`).

The office Macs (glaeda minis and ultras) share one egress address, and github.com answers 403 to its anonymous
requests. setup-bun's download of `bun-darwin-aarch64.zip` is anonymous, and the Macs' own `~/.bun/bin/bun` is
1.3.6, so setup-bun downloads 1.4.4 on every run. Each Mac job of cmux-next then failed at Setup Bun with a 403
and no test run: on the feat-cmux-next base tier, 2026-11-25 14:38Z to 2026-11-26 07:02Z, daemon tests were green
0 of 5, swift test 0 of 1 and generated files (Mac fold) 1 of 9 (jobs 139739263484, 139745965090, 139727014744).

`toolchain-r2.sh restore` ran only on Freestyle runners (`fsr-*`), so on these Macs it said "not a Freestyle
runner" and did nothing. `TOOLCHAIN_R2_ALL=1` runs it on every runner. With #58009 (restore on Darwin, the darwin
archives pinned and seeded) the Mac puts the pinned bun at `~/.bun/bin/bun` from the bucket, checked against its
sha256, and setup-bun finds it and skips its download. A miss, a mismatch or an unreachable bucket is exit 0 and
setup-bun runs as before. Before #58009 lands the script still says "Linux only" on a Mac and does nothing; on a
Linux runner it now restores bun from the bucket instead of downloading from GitHub, the same as nightly and
backend.yml already do.
