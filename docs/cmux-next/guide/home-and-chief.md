# Home and Chief

The Home screen, Chief, and group chats.

Home is the first screen. It looks like a messages app. Each row is a conversation: a Chief, a person on your team, or a group chat.

Chief is your assistant. You ask in plain words. Chief starts agents, opens panes, and reports back in the same thread.

## Commands

```bash
cmux home open-chief      # open Chief
cmux home tab             # open Home in a tab
cmux home new-chief       # make another Chief
cmux home send            # send a message
```

## Teammates

People in your team show up as contacts. You can message one, or start a group chat with several people and Chiefs.

In a group chat, use Hide Alerts to mute, Pin/Unpin Conversation to keep it on top, and Leave Group to leave.
