# What a chat's view reads, and where it comes from with `chat` on

Today the page builds `AcpmuxSnapshot` and its `AcpmuxRow`s from raw acpmux events (direct.ts). With the `chat` stream on, the daemon sends a window of rendered rows and four ops (`row`, `text`, `patch`, `queue`), and the page keeps its v1 connection for everything that is not the chat's rows. This file lists every field of the two types, says whether the pane reads it, and names the one place it would come from with `chat` on. Where nothing on the v2 wire carries a field, it says "no source".

Paths and conventions:

- **Readers** are under `webviews/src/agent-session/acpmux/` on feat-cmux-next. Readers in direct.ts (the producer), galleries, mocks, fixtures, debug.ts and automation.ts are not counted as the chat view.
- **Wire** files: `row.rs` is `cmux-tui/crates/acpmux-book/src/row.rs`. `wire.rs` and `chat.rs` are cmux#43130's, at its head `ba063da` (`cmux-tui/crates/acpmux-client/src/`). The list types are `cmux-tui/crates/acpmux-list-types/src/lib.rs` (cited `list lib.rs`). The list's `extra` keys are in `cmux-tui/crates/acpmux/src/hub/list_tee/summary.rs` (cited `list_tee`).
- **Translator** files are `cmux-tui/crates/acpmux-book/src/translate/{mod,v1,claude,input}.rs`. A body key is either a field the translator sets (`row.field`/`fields.insert`), or a `BodyField` the actor writes under the name `acpmux-core/src/chat.rs:1521-1531` gives it (`text`, `content`, `input`, `entries`, `source`, `images`, `title`).
- **Row head**: `row.id` (u64, row.rs:97), `row.rev` (99), `row.kind` (100), `row.turn` (the rowId of the turn's prompt, 102), `row.group` (104), `row.at` (seconds, 106), `row.open` (108), `row.body` (110), `row.est` (112), `row.blob{bytes,preview}` (251-257).
- **"v1 connection"** means the pane's request/notification channel (initialize, session summary, status, asks, settings) without the chat's raw event stream. Events that only the stream carried, and that the translator drops, are "no source".
- **"page-made"** marks a field the page computes today from other rows or from its own state (mostly conversation/turns.ts). Nothing on the wire carries it, and nothing has to unless the daemon takes that shaping over. These fields are listed under "No source" in their own group.
