## What this is

This is a CI check (**gallery-feel**) that runs a simulated cursor over **every gallery state** without a script. For each state it:

- finds the visible, enabled, hit-testable controls (buttons, links, roles, draggables, separators and sliders)
- moves the cursor to each one in steps of about 16 px and hovers it
- clicks it, or drags it 40 px
- presses Escape

Then it judges what happened:

| finding | rule |
|---|---|
| dead click | a click that changes nothing (no DOM change, no focus move, no scroll) |
| slow response | the first visible change comes more than 200 ms after a click or drag (the INP "good" bound) |
| pop-in | a later wave of content, at least 100 ms after the first response, adds a node of at least 64 px² |
| layout jump | a click causes a layout shift after its first wave plus 50 ms |
| hover shift | a hover causes any layout shift |

A state is re-crawled once to drop flakes. Findings are checked against `scripts/gallery-matrix/feel-baseline.json`: the check fails only on new findings. Known findings and fixed ones are listed in the step summary.

**Split with #50132 (feel-harness lane, agreed 14:21Z):** #50132 owns the scripted per-surface feel tests in `webviews/test/feel/`. This PR adds the unscripted crawl over every gallery state, so surfaces nobody has written a script for get graded too. Once #50132 lands, a follow-up switches the thresholds to its `verdicts.ts` and, where it fits, records through its `probe.ts`, so a dead click or pop-in gets the same number in both.

## Files

- `scripts/gallery-matrix/feel.ts`: the in-page probe, the crawl and the judges
- `scripts/gallery-matrix/feel.test.ts`: 13 unit tests of the judges
- `scripts/gallery-matrix/feel-browser.test.ts`: a positive control. It serves a page built to feel bad and asserts every finding with its number (the slow button reads 320.9 ms), and none on the good button. It's gated on `CMUX_GALLERY_IN_VM=1`, so it runs in fast-checks, which installs Chrome.
- `.github/workflows/gallery-feel.yml`: runs on PRs that touch `webviews/src/**`. It crawls the touched gallery entries (at most 12 entries and 60 states), or everything on dispatch or when the feel files change, and uploads `feel.json`.

## Detector and meta-problem

- **Detector:** `scripts/gallery-matrix/feel.ts`, run by the `gallery-feel` check.
- **Meta-problem:** feel was graded only where someone had scripted a play (`webviews/src/gallery/play.ts`) or a per-surface test. Any control without a script went ungraded, so dead clicks and pop-in shipped silently. This crawl grades every control the gallery renders.
