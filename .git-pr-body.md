## What changes

**Shaped split preview.** The sidebar's workspace hover card draws a miniature of the workspace's real split layout: one rounded tile per pane at its real share of the screen, a mark for each pane's front tab (terminal, page or agent) and the focused pane outlined in the accent. Scrolling-column screens draw their columns at their widths. A one-pane or zoomed workspace draws no miniature. Ratios are rounded to hundredths, so dragging a divider does not re-map the row on every point.
- `SidebarPaneShape` (model plus frame layout), `SidebarMapping.paneShape` (from the daemon's screen tree) and `WorkspacePaneShapeView` (drawing; VoiceOver reads "Panes: N").

**One decided + menu.** Right-clicking the + button, or opening its chooser, shows only the rows that make something: New Workspace, New Browser Workspace, Open Folder, and so on. The sidebar's show/hide view rows stay on the empty sidebar background's menu, not on the + button.

**No Remove from Top / Add to Top.** A top row's menu (the Chief's included) no longer offers Remove from Top, and a workspace row's menu no longer offers Add to Top. Rows are dragged into and out of the top rows; both drags already exist (`dropWorkspaces` / `userBandEdit`). `workspace.toggleTop` stays in the palette and CLI, with a context-menu exemption of `dragGesture`. A pinned tile keeps Unpin Workspace.
