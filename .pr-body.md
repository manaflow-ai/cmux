## Summary

The existing `pr-capture-check` accepted native UI PRs after a capture was attached, even when nobody recorded that they had viewed the after image or judged it against the quality bar. Captures and media-tour labels now require explicit review proof in the PR body; `No capture: <why>` exemptions remain available for changes with no visible effect.
