cmux is where you talk to your coding agents from your iPhone or iPad.

Message a Chief: an agent that takes a task, runs coding agents on your own machines, and messages you when it is done or needs you. Your Chiefs and the people you work with share one Home, so an update from an agent reads like a text from a teammate.

- Home keeps every conversation in one list. Pin, mute, mark as read, and search your messages.
- Start a New Chief and name it for the work it owns, like Release or Infra.
- Start a New Group to bring people and Chiefs into one conversation.
- Invite someone to cmux by email or text message.
- Get a notification the moment a Chief needs you. Reply from the banner, or Allow or Deny an agent's request. Notifications are optional.
- Call your Chief or record a voice note for it with Siri, Shortcuts, or the Action Button.
- In Chat Server, keep cmux Cloud with your cmux account, or choose Self-Hosted to use your own server on your network.

Sign in with Apple, Google, GitHub, or a one-time code sent to your email.

Your agents run on your own machines with cmux, not on your phone.
