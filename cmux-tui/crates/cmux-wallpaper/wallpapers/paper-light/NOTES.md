Afternoon: point of view, afternoon sun on a desk. A sheet of paper with a warm, slanted pool of sun on it, shaped by the window it came through.
Paper: fixed fiber noise in points (so it looks the same on any screen): value noise stretched 8:1 to 10:1 at three angles, each read as its ridge cubed, so the grain is thin bright strands 6 to 20 pt long rather than blobs; a per-pixel tooth hash and a faint low cockle. No time term: the paper never shimmers.
Light: the pool is a rounded box in rotated, sheared coordinates (a window projected by low sun). Its penumbra widens toward the pool's far (lower) side, from 0.6x to 1.9x, as real sunlight does with distance from the edge that casts it. Inside it, the window: Venetian (slat shadows, 60% lit, a little out of parallel, sagging toward the middle, swaying a hair on a 23 s breath), Pane (one upright and two glazing bars of a sash, soft shadows), Leaves (a larger, three times softer pool dappled by 4-octave fbm, clusters stirring on small local orbits of 6 to 8 s, many directions at once, never one coherent flow). A faint bounce glow surrounds the pool.
Motion: the pool's center and shear drift on incommensurate periods of about ten minutes (`drift` scales them), well under a point a second; thin clouds dim the sun by up to 14% over half a minute or more (slow 1D noise in time). The raking light lifts the paper's grain where it falls (up to 2.6x), and the shadow side leans faintly cool (sky fill) against the warm sun.
Color: OKLCH, mixed in OKLab, dithered +-1/255 (triangular PDF, gamma-2.2 space, static). Light: paper L 0.925 warm, lit to about L 0.98 with chroma 0.032 at hue 72. Dark: paper L 0.205, the same sun a little stronger (a low sun in a dim room).
Variants: Venetian (11 slats, sun from the left), Pane (sash, sun from the right, softer), Leaves (dappled).
Pointer: none; `interactive` is on so pointer motion keeps the slow drift running (idle_after 20).
Still: time 40 s; looks complete as one frame.
Cost per pixel: fibers (3 noises with rotations, ~110 ALU), tooth, cockle and cloud (~70), two box distances (~30), the window (blinds or pane ~25; leaves a 4-octave fbm and a noise ~150), three OKLCH colors (~30), OKLab to linear and the dither (~70): ~350 ALU (blinds, pane), ~480 (leaves). At scale 0.5 a 1440 x 900 point window on a 2x screen is 1.3 M pixels, 0.45 to 0.63 G ops a frame at 20 fps: estimated 0.2 to 0.3 ms on an M1-class GPU; not measured on a Mac yet.
Params (8): mode (0 blinds, 1 pane, 2 leaves), light, softness (penumbra), slats, angle (shear), grain, drift, warmth.
