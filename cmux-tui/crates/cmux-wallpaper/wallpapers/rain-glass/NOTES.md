Rain: a rainy window seen from a warm room. Point of view: inside, warm, while it rains.
Behind the glass: an out-of-focus city. A sky-to-street gradient (OKLab) and two bokeh layers from hash grids (far: 9 cells per height, near: 3.6, at 0.55 weight), soft discs with a slightly brighter rim, only below a soft horizon, colored from the palette's few lights (City: sodium, warm white, tail-light red, one teal sign; Neon: magenta, cyan, violet, a little sodium; Dawn: warm whites, amber, a few cool street lights). Each light breathes on its own period of 50 to 150 s; nothing pans. Each layer checks only the 2 x 2 nearest cells: disc radius plus jitter stays within one cell, so no other cell can reach the pixel.
On the glass: a fine mist (46 cells per height, about half filled, 2-6 px drops at scale 0.5) and larger static drops (10 per height, ~38% filled), one per cell at most, radius and jitter kept inside the cell. Each grows in over ~5% of its 70-200 s life (several seconds, never a one-frame onset) and evaporates over the last 20%. In columns 0.11 heights wide, about half the columns per cycle carry a sliding drop: it falls 0.034 heights a second on average in stick-slip steps (seven pauses a cycle, the speed never below 0.1x nor above 1.9x, so at most ~0.04 widths/s at 16:10), along a fixed meander, from above the window to well below it. Behind it a wet trail: clear of fog and of the mist and static drops it absorbed, beaded with tiny droplets that evaporate as the trail fades (e-folding 0.45 heights behind the drop); the trail is gone before the column's next drop starts, so nothing pops at the cycle's wrap.
Every drop is a small lens: the pixel looks up the city once, at the drop's center minus 5x its radius-scaled offset, so the drop shows a minified, inverted city; drops are clear (no fog), with a dark rim and a warm glint from the lamp in the room (upper left). Fog on the rest of the glass (22-32%, uneven, fixed) softens the city; a lamp's reflection warms the lower right and a soft vignette deepens dark mode.
Light and dark (presence.w): each palette has a light (overcast day, pale dawn) and a dark (night) sky; lights add in linear light, so on a light sky they are faint tinted discs and at night soft glows. Dark sky L 0.18-0.30, lights peak around L 0.40-0.45 at their centers; fog pulls the scene toward L 0.30 (dark) or 0.90 (light). Triangular dither +-1/255 in a gamma-2 space.
Pointer: none (interactive false). Typing still wakes it (the scheduler's rule), and it holds after 20 s of nothing.
Still (time.w = 1): time 40 s (Neon 63, Dawn 25) shows sliding drops mid-window with trails, mist and static drops at various sizes. Complete as a single frame.
Cost per pixel: sliding drop and its path (hash2, hash, 2 x 2 sin, ellipse) ~70 ALU; trail droplet cell (hash2, 2 sin, 2 exp) ~60; two static drop layers (2 hash2, a division and a length each) ~100; the city: one sky palette branch (2 OKLCH colors) and OKLab to linear ~45, two bokeh layers x 4 cells x (2 hash2, length, 2 smoothsteps, sin, a palette pick) ~60 = ~480; fog (OKLCH, conversion, one value noise) ~90; drop shading, glint, lamp and vignette (2 exp, length) ~45; dither ~25. Total ~950 ALU per pixel, no textures, fixed 2 x 2 loops.
Budget: scale 0.5, fps 20. A 1440 x 900 point window on a 2x screen renders 1.3 M pixels, ~1.2 G ops a frame, estimated ~0.5 ms on an M1-class GPU (~10 ms of GPU per second at 20 fps); not measured on a Mac yet. The mist needs scale 0.5 to stay drops; if it measures over 0.5 ms, drop the near bokeh layer to 2 x 1 cells or fps to 15.
Params (6): palette (0 City, 1 Neon, 2 Dawn), rain (drop density), speed, city lights, fog on the glass, lamp warmth. idle_after 20.
