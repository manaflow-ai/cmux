Dot Field: a hex-packed lattice of fine dots (rows sqrt(3)/2 pitch apart, every other row offset half a pitch) in the theme's ink at low contrast on its paper; works in light and dark (presence.w: ink mixed in linear light reads fainter on a light paper, so the light theme gets 1.8x the ink for the same apparent contrast, and in dark the halo adds light instead of tinting the paper).
Dot size at rest: a fixed, seed-based value-noise field over the lattice (scale 7 cells), `variation` deep; there is no time term, so a frame at rest is the same frame whenever it is drawn, and the held frame (idle_after 2, so the display link stops 2 s after the last move or key) is exactly the live frame.
Pointer: reads `Frame.trail` (16 rows, newest first, about 50 ms apart, ~0.8 s of path; src/trail.rs). For each of the 15 segments it takes the distance from the dot's lattice center to the segment and the age interpolated along it; energy = max over segments of exp(-d^2/R^2 - age * decay), read through a square root so the older part of the path stays visible, with a smooth cutoff from 0.7 to 1.0 s of age so a point that expires at 1 s never pops, times presence.x and a head fade on presence.y (0.6..1.0 s since moved).
Lit dots grow toward 0.42 cell (so they stay separate in a cell whose inscribed radius is 0.5), lean from ink to the accent with a small soft halo that ends before the cell's edge, and part from the path: a dot beside the path moves away from the nearest point of the strongest segment by up to 0.2 cell (`push`), never past its own cell, while a dot on the path stays put, like filings parting.
A still (time.w = 1) synthesizes a gentle arc of 16 points ending at the still pointer with ages 0..0.8 s, so the cover shows the effect.
Cost per pixel: one fixed loop of 15 segments, no textures. Lattice ~20 ALU; size noise 4 hashes ~55 ALU; dot, push and halo ~40 ALU with 1 exp and 1 sqrt. Each segment whose two rows are valid costs ~30 ALU with 1 exp; a segment with an invalid row is skipped by a uniform branch (the rows are uniform), so at rest (no valid rows) the loop costs ~30 ALU of compares: ~150 ALU per pixel at rest, ~570 ALU and 17 exp with a full trail. A still adds a cos and sin per row.
At scale 0.5 a 1440 x 900 point window on a 2x screen is 1.3 M pixels: ~0.75 G ops a frame with a full trail, estimated well under 1 ms on an M1-class GPU (the budget is 4 ms), and ~0.2 G ops at rest; not measured on a Mac yet.
Params (8): pitch in points (times backing scale, floor 3 px), dot size (cell fraction), trail radius in cells, fade rate (1/s), glow (how far a lit dot goes), push (parting, 0..1 of 0.2 cell), field contrast, size variation.
