Pencil Grid: point of view, quiet is a texture. After Agnes Martin: ruled pencil lines on warm paper inside a margin (6.5% of the shorter side), horizontals close together (13 pt), fainter verticals wider apart (39 pt, 0 turns them off), so the grid is of small wide rectangles.
Each line is drawn by hand against a ruler. It wobbles a fraction of a point over long runs (1D value noise at 110 pt, plus a little at 11 pt); its pressure is set per line and swells and fades along it (noise at 170 pt); its ends land and lift a few points unevenly, each with a short taper. The graphite catches only on the paper's tooth (fixed noise at 0.75 pt plus a per-pixel hash): a light stroke leaves only the peaks and breaks into grain, a firm one fills in. Line profile is a Gaussian, sigma 0.33 pt (at least 0.55 rendered px, so it never aliases). The paper has the same tooth at 0.6% L and a faint cockle.
Light: a very wide, soft band (Gaussian, 0.34 height) on a slight slant crosses the window in about 140 s (0.012 widths a second), off the window between passes so the wrap is never seen. It lifts the paper (2.4% L in light, 1.6% in dark, a touch warm) and makes the graphite glint: the lines get 28% less dark and their tone lighter where it passes.
Pointer: every other row of Frame.trail (7 segments, newest first); lines within about 30 pt of the recent path darken up to 55%, fading with the path's age (gone by 1 s) and with presence, as if just drawn. Segments with an invalid row are skipped by a uniform branch, so at rest the loop is nearly free. A still draws no trail.
Palettes (OKLCH, mixed in OKLab, dithered +-1/255, static): Graphite (warm paper L 0.935, cool graphite; dark: deep warm ground L 0.215 with pale silvery lines), Night (dark: blue-black ground L 0.19 with pale lines; light: a cool gray-blue paper with blue-gray lines), Rose (near-white and pale pink washed bands, 9 to the height, soft and wandering at the edges, under warm gray lines, no verticals; dark: deep rose-brown bands). Light themes get more graphite (0.34 vs 0.26) for the same presence.
Still: time 67 s puts the light band just right of center; looks complete as one frame.
Cost per pixel: tooth, cockle and band noise (~90 ALU), three palette lookups (~30), two horizontal and two vertical lines at ~105 ALU each (two wobble noises, a pressure noise, three hashes, an exp, three smoothsteps), the trail (7 x ~25, only while it is valid), OKLab to linear and the dither (~70): ~620 ALU at rest, ~800 with a full trail, 4 to 11 exp. At scale 0.5 a 1440 x 900 point window on a 2x screen is 1.3 M pixels (one rendered pixel per point), ~0.8 to 1.0 G ops a frame at 20 fps: estimated ~0.35 to 0.45 ms on an M1-class GPU; not measured on a Mac yet.
Params (8): pitch (points), vpitch (points, 0 none), weight, wobble, grain, light (band), palette (0 Graphite, 1 Night, 2 Rose), bands (Rose wash).
