Rothko Field: point of view, a window can hold a feeling. A ground of deep color with two (or three) stacked fields on it, inside side and top margins, after Rothko's stacked rectangles; the window's wide aspect lays them out wide, top field the largest.
The edge is the picture. Each field's boundary is a rounded-box distance pushed about by three things from one shared noise field (the fields sit in different places, so one field serves every edge): slow fbm lumps (3 octaves, 0.017 height units deep), a dry-brush streak (value noise stretched 10:1 along the edge, horizontal on the long edges and vertical on the short ones, blended at the corners) and a feather width that itself varies along the edge (0.7x to 1.3x). Where the paint thins over the ground, a faint lighter halo halfway between the two colors sits just outside the field (scumble). Interiors are never flat: a low wash noise moves each field and the ground about 1% L.
Motion: the noise field sways on a 97 s circle (a few points of edge movement, nothing slides), and each field breathes in luminance (about 1.1% L) and edge softness (+-20%) on its own period and phase (10.0 s, 11.7 s, 9.3 s; the ground 13.1 s, 0.4% L), about 6 a minute and never in step, so it does not tick like a metronome. Fastest luminance change is under 1% L a second. Phases start at the window's seed.
Pointer: the pointer inside or within 0.2 height of a field warms the whole field very slightly (OKLab +0.011 a, +0.009 b, +0.007 L at full `warmth`), eased by presence.x, falling off smoothly with distance, so it never pops and reads as the field warming, not a spotlight.
Color: palettes are authored in OKLCH, mixed in OKLab, converted to linear sRGB and dithered (triangular PDF, +-1/255 in a gamma-2.2 space, static in screen space). Dark keeps the grounds in L 0.21 to 0.27 and fields at or under L 0.375; light lifts the ground to L 0.80 to 0.86 and keeps each field's relation to it (Seagram's fields darker than the ground, Saffron's lighter), at 85% chroma. A canvas tooth (fixed noise, 0.7% L) gives the paint something to sit on.
Variants: Seagram (maroon ground, black-oxblood and oxblood fields, three fields), No. 61 (deep blue ground, a glowing blue field over a rust one), Saffron (red ground, orange over yellow ochre).
Still: time 6 s, pointer at the upper right over the top field; looks complete as one frame (the breath is a small luminance offset).
Cost per pixel: one 3-octave fbm and 5 value noises (~230 ALU), three fields at ~70 ALU each (two box distances, an exp, a smoothstep, a palette lookup with a cos and sin), OKLab to linear and the dither (~70 ALU): ~550 ALU, 3 exp, ~10 trig. At scale 0.5 a 1440 x 900 point window on a 2x screen is 1.3 M pixels, ~0.7 G ops a frame at 20 fps: estimated ~0.3 ms on an M1-class GPU (target 0.5 ms); not measured on a Mac yet.
Params (8): palette (0 Seagram, 1 No. 61, 2 Saffron), breath, feather (edge softness), brush (edge noise and wash), warmth (pointer), count (2 or 3 fields), chroma, canvas grain.
