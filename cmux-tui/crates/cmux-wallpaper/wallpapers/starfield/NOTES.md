Night Sky (starfield): POV: depth behind the work. Four star layers from hash grids, one candidate star per cell: dust (6 pt cells, faintest), far (15 pt), mid (38 pt), near (90 pt, with a soft halo on the brightest). Each star: a jittered position within 0.3 cell of its cell's center, a steep power-law magnitude (h^7, so bright stars are rare), a color temperature from orange through white to blue at low OKLab chroma (most near white), and a core Gaussian whose energy is kept below a pixel (no aliasing, no crawl). The glow ends inside the cell, so one cell per layer.
Milky band: a soft diagonal strip shaped by 4-octave fbm with a darker rift along it; it lifts the sky a little (dark) or shades it (light) and makes the dust and far layers fuller inside it. Sky: the theme's paper leaning toward the `zenith` hue at the top and the accent at the bottom (horizon glow), mixed in OKLab; on a light paper the same lean reads as dawn (Dawn Sky), and stars become fine colored specks instead of added light.
Motion: the whole sky turns about a pole 1.4 heights above the window at `drift` degrees a minute (0.25: about 0.15 px/s at the bottom of a 900 px window; you notice it only minutes later). Stars are at infinity, so the turn has no parallax. Twinkle: about one star in three swells (sin^18 of a 25..75 s period, ~3 s wide), brighter by up to 0.8 x `twinkle`: rare, slow, never a strobe. Look-around: the pointer's offset from the window's center shifts the layers by depth (dust 0.05, far 0.2, mid 0.5, near 1.0 of `parallax` points, eased with presence.x). This is the only input coupling; the synthesis lists parallax as risky, so it is small (10 pt) and depth-scaled, and 0 turns it off.
Dither: TPDF +-1 LSB in the encoded space (static), so the band and horizon never band.
Still (time 40): the sky as is, the look-around at the still pointer.
Cost per pixel: rotation ~10 ALU (1 sin/cos pair uniform); band: 4 octaves of value noise (16 hashes) ~180 ALU with 1 exp; 4 star layers, each 1 hash42 + Gaussian + halo + twinkle ~45 ALU with 2 exp, 1 pow, 1 sin (~180); OKLab conversions and sky ~120; dither ~45: ~550 ALU per pixel.
At scale 0.5 a 1440 x 900 pt window on a 2x screen is 1.3 M pixels: ~0.7 G ops a frame, estimated ~0.4-0.5 ms on an M1-class GPU, at 20 fps (its motion is very slow); idle_after 20. Not measured on a Mac yet.
Params (8): density, brightness, milky band, twinkle (0..1), drift (degrees per minute), look-around (points), horizon glow, zenith hue (degrees).
