Skyspace: point of view, one color, looked at long enough, becomes an event. A square aperture (rounded up to a circle by `round`) in a flat ceiling, after Turrell's skyspaces, with a knife edge: one rendered pixel of antialiasing and no rim, so the sky reads as a solid plane of color laid on the ceiling rather than a hole.
Sky: a nearly flat gradient, zenith at the upper left of the opening easing to a faint horizon glow at the lower right, plus a very low, very slow noise swell (0.8% L) so the plane is not dead flat. Its color walks four keys (zenith and horizon each), Dusk: afternoon blue, sunset violet over peach, dusk indigo over rose, night; the walk is a ping-pong (0.5 - 0.5 cos), so there is no seam where it wraps, one full there-and-back every `cycle` minutes (24 by default), starting at a per-window place (the seed) so two windows sit at different hours. At 24 minutes the field changes well under 0.002 OKLab deltaE a second.
Ceiling: the theme's paper, converted to OKLab and tinted by the sky's own light, a little everywhere (10% of the sky's a/b) and more near the opening (up to 32%), with a faint lift by the lip in dark (light spilling over) and a faint dimming in light; a vignette darkens the window's corners slightly. That tinted boundary is the Turrell trick: the frame's color is what makes the sky's color read.
Ganzfeld (frame 0): no aperture; the whole window is the field, the gradient runs top to bottom, with a soft bloom at the center and falloff toward the edges.
Light and dark: keys are authored for dark (sky L 0.2 to 0.47); light maps L to 0.56 + 0.7 L at 85% chroma (sky L 0.70 to 0.89 under a bright ceiling, the skyspace at dusk with its lights on), so dark text still reads over it. OKLab throughout, triangular-PDF dither of +-1/255 in a gamma-2.2 space, static in screen space.
Variants: Dusk (24 min, rounded square), Blue Hour (30 min, circle, more spill), Ganzfeld (16 min, no frame, magenta, blue and rose fields). Each has its own still time, set near the middle of its cycle (sunset).
Pointer: none; `interactive` is on only so pointer motion keeps the slow cycle running (idle_after 20 holds it otherwise).
Cost per pixel: the two sky colors (4 keys each, a cos and sin per key, ~180 ALU; uniform per frame, but the program has no per-frame stage), one value noise (~30), a box distance, OKLab both ways (~60 with a cube-root pow) and the dither (~40): ~360 ALU, 16 trig, 1 exp. At scale 0.5 a 1440 x 900 point window on a 2x screen is 1.3 M pixels, ~0.47 G ops a frame at 15 fps: estimated ~0.2 ms on an M1-class GPU; not measured on a Mac yet.
Params (8): cycle (minutes), aperture (fraction of the height), round, frame (0 Ganzfeld .. 1), palette (0 Dusk, 1 Blue Hour, 2 Ganzfeld), spill (sky light on the frame), edge (0 knife .. 1 soft), vignette.
