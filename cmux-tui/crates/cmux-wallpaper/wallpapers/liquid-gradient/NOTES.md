# Liquid Gradient

- Reference: the blurred, wiggled shapes that morph into flowing gradient blobs in "5 Cinematic Motion Graphics You Should Know in After Effects" (Leo: "after effects level animations in your background").
- Moves: five blobs, each a wide Gaussian whose center rides a Lissajous path over the whole window (rates 0.067 to 0.25 rad per unit time, irrational multiples of each other, hashed per window seed) plus a wiggle of two faster, smaller sines per axis. A low-frequency sine warp of the whole field bends the blobs so they morph rather than stay round.
- Field: the Gaussians' sum is the metaball field; coverage is `1 - exp(-1.7 * sum)`, so the paper shows between blobs with no edge. Colors are weighted by each Gaussian squared, so where blobs meet the colors melt into each other.
- Color: mixed in a sqrt (perceptual) space. Ground is the paper; blobs are the accent, the accent turned `spread` and `-0.8 * spread` about the gray axis, an accent-ink mix (35% ink) and a deep accent turned `1.7 * spread`. Light themes pull the blob colors 18% toward the paper (tints); dark themes take 85% of `intensity`. Default intensity 0.42 keeps it low-contrast under code.
- Pointer: each blob's center moves up to 22% of the way toward the pointer (times `lean`), weighted by `exp(-2 d^2)`, eased by `presence.x`.
- Agents (`agents: true`): each event advances the flow by `3 * response` time units, eased over 2 s (`activity.z + smoothstep(0, 2, activity.y)`, continuous across events; it steps once per 1024 events when the count wraps). Multiplying time by the level was not used: that jumps the phase by `time * delta` at large times. The level lifts the accent blob's saturation (up to +45%) and brightness (+18%) and grows the warp; a new event swells every blob's radius by up to 28% and the intensity by 25%, fading as `exp(-1.1 * activity.y)`. A still shows level 0.4 and no swell.
- Banding: a triangular dither one 8-bit step wide (two integer hashes, new each frame) in the sqrt space, which is close to sRGB, plus optional grain (`grain * 0.03`). Checked at 12x contrast: no bands.
- Cost: one loop of exactly 5 blobs. Per blob: 2 hash sines, 6 sin for the path and wiggle, 1 exp for the lean, 1 exp for the Gaussian, about 45 ALU. Outside: 2 sin warp, 3 hue turns (1 sin/cos pair each, ~20 ALU), 3 sqrt (vec3), 1 exp coverage, 2 integer hashes. About 330 ALU ops and ~55 transcendentals per pixel, constant in size and input. The cheapness lever is `scale: 0.35`: the content is all blur, so 0.35 looks the same as 1.0 for about an eighth of the pixels (a 1512x982 pt window on a 2x screen is about 0.73 MP per frame at 30 fps). No textures, no branches but uniform selects.
- Variants: Liquid (default, blue), Aurora (green, larger blobs, more wiggle and hue spread), Dusk (orange, smaller blobs, more grain).
