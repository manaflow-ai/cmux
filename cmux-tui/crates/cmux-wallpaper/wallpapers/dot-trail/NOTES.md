Comet Dust (dot-trail): the refined pointer-trail field. POV: the room notices you. A square grid of dust-sized dots (~1 pt across, `pitch` 8 pt), each nudged up to `jitter` of a cell by a per-cell hash so it reads as dust, not graph paper, with a fixed per-dot brightness variation; at rest the field is almost invisible (`contrast` 0.09 in dark, 1.7x in light for the same apparent contrast). No time term at rest: the held frame (idle_after 2.5) is exactly the live frame.
Dots are Gaussians whose energy is kept when they are smaller than a pixel (peak scaled by (r/sigma)^2, sigma >= 0.6 px), so a 1 pt dot at scale 0.5 on a 1x screen never aliases or crawls; it just gets fainter.
Pointer: reads `Frame.trail` exactly like dot-field (16 rows, newest first, ~50 ms apart; 15 segments; empty segments skipped by a uniform branch). Per pixel, per segment: distance to the segment and the age along it. `core` is a tube of radius `radius` (30 pt) that widens from 0.75x at the head to 1.5x at the end as it cools; `wide` is the same path at twice the radius (the core Gaussian to the 1/4, two sqrt, no extra exp): the analytic bloom, the light bleeding onto the paper and the neighbouring dots. The fade uses the trail window: full until `linger` (a fraction of 0.88 s), then a smoothstep to zero at 0.88 s, just before a full ring drops its oldest point (~0.8 s of travel) or a row expires at 1 s, so the tail never pops off; times presence.x.
Color, all in OKLab by the age at the strongest `wide` point: hot at the head (toward white on a dark paper, deeper and fuller on a light one), the accent through the first third, then the accent's hue turned by `shift` degrees and dimmed: the afterglow (Ember -35: orange to crimson; Glacier +45: ice to deep blue; Aurora +120: green to violet). Lit dots grow to ~2.4x and go to that color; in dark the bloom adds light (linear), in light it tints the paper.
Sparkle: dots with a high per-dot hash near the head (a tighter Gaussian on the head row) shimmer at ~1 Hz and go white-hot (dark) or deep (light); gone with the head. Awareness: around the pointer (2.5 trail radii) the resting field shows a little more ink, so after the trail fades the room still knows where you are.
Dither: TPDF +-1 LSB in the encoded space (static hash, no time term), so the soft bloom never bands.
A still (time.w = 1) draws its own long arc of 16 points ending at the still pointer, ages 0..0.95 s, so the cover shows the whole head-to-afterglow gradient.
Cost per pixel: lattice and 2 hashes ~35 ALU; trail: head ~30 ALU with 2 exp, each valid segment ~35 ALU with 1 exp and 2 sqrt (15 segments ~525); color: 3 OKLab conversions (paper, ink, accent: 3 pow each) ~110, gradient ~45, 2 OKLab-to-linear ~50, dither ~45 with 2 pow: ~850 ALU per pixel with a full trail, ~330 at rest (the loop costs only compares then).
At scale 0.5 a 1440 x 900 pt window on a 2x screen is 1.3 M pixels: ~1.1 G ops a frame with a full trail, estimated ~0.6-0.8 ms on an M1-class GPU (over the 0.5 ms target only while the pointer moves; it holds 2.5 s after), ~0.3 ms at rest. Not measured on a Mac yet.
Params (8): pitch (pt), dot radius (pt), trail radius (pt), linger (0..0.9 of the 1 s window held at full), glow, afterglow hue shift (degrees), field contrast, jitter (0..0.25 cell).
