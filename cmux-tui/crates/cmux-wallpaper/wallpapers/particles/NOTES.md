# Particles

- Moves: one hashed particle per grid cell, jittered, each wandering on its own slow sine pair (hash-picked frequencies 0.06-0.17 rad/s, so no shared period) while the whole field drifts linearly; a 2-octave value-noise dust cloud drifts at a different rate and gates which particles exist, so density slowly morphs. Particles are soft blobs thresholded per pixel by ordered 4x4 Bayer or interleaved-gradient (blue-noise-like) dither; sparse hashed haze specks and a 2% paper grain sit between them.
- Pointer: an inverse warp in an ellipse metric around the pointer: r0 = r - (R - c) e^{-(r - R)/W}. Inside R nothing shows (the calm clearing); the source disc inside c is pushed out of sight; the band outside is squeezed into a denser rim; far out the map is the identity. Each particle's distance is corrected by the map's local radial squeeze and tangential stretch, so pushed grains stay round. Pointer velocity stretches the ellipse along the motion and sets its centre back, so the clearing trails into a wake and closes as the host's smoothed velocity and presence ease out.
- Accent: only particles whose source lies just outside c (they now line the rim), and of those only a hashed share (params.accent, 0.14-0.3), drawn slightly larger in the accent color. Nothing else uses it.
- Variants: Dust (fine 12 pt cells, blue-noise dither, cloudy), Star field (sparse 22 pt cells, small points, ordered dither, flat), Nebula (dense 9 pt cells, larger grains, strong cloud contrast, wider clearing, more stretch).
- Cost per pixel: no loop beyond the fixed 3x3 cell neighbourhood (9 iterations). Per pixel: ~40 flops + 1 exp for the warp, 2 value-noise octaves (8 cell hashes), 2 hashes for haze and grain, the dither threshold. Per neighbour: 3 integer hashes, 1 sin + 1 cos, 1 exp, 2 lengths, ~60 flops. Total roughly 1.3k ALU ops, 9 exp and 18 sin/cos per pixel; no textures, no branches beyond selects. At 3.7 M px x 30 fps that is about 150 GFLOP/s, a few percent of an Apple-silicon GPU.
