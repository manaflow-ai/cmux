Marble: a polished slab of stone. Point of view: stone that breathes.
What it draws: Quilez-style double domain warp. q = fbm2(p), r = fbm2(p + 2.2 warp q), s = fbm(p + 2.6 warp r) (fbm2 is a 3-octave vector value-noise fbm, one hash pass per octave for both channels; s is a 4-octave scalar fbm; octaves rotate ~37 degrees so lattice axes never line up). The body is two close tones clouded by q.x + r.x. The veins are warped level lines: a main vein where s = 0.5 (bent by q.x), a thinner second level at s = 0.63, and grey hairlines where r.y crosses zero, gated to part of the slab so the stone keeps quiet passages. Because the warp's gradient varies, veins swell and thin along their length on their own.
Subsurface: every main vein has a wide, faint halo of its own mineral (a Gaussian ~5x the core's width at 0.55 weight), which reads as color seen a little way into the polished stone. The halos breathe: +-12% at a ~10.5 s period with a slowly wandering phase (no metronome), about +-0.005 L at most.
Motion: only the warp's inner offsets move (0.006 noise units per second at drift 1, a few points a second), so the veins creep and re-route rather than slide; nothing translates as a whole.
Sheen: a polished slab is flat, so its reflection needs no normals: a broad Gaussian (0.47 heights) plus a tighter one (0.14), +0.028 L in light and +0.045 L in dark at the peak, a little desaturated, weaker on the veins (less polish). At rest it sits upper left (0.28 width, 0.22 height); while the pointer is over the window it eases there with presence.x, so the light follows the hand without jumping.
Light and dark (presence.w): each stone has a light and a dark palette, authored in OKLCH and mixed in OKLab. Calacatta: warm white with gold veins and grey hairlines (dark: warm charcoal with dim gold). Nero: black with white veins (light: dove grey with charcoal veins). Verde: deep green with pale jade veins (light: celadon with deep green veins). Dark bodies sit at L 0.15-0.24, veins at most L 0.50; light bodies at L 0.80-0.955, veins no darker than L 0.50. Triangular dither +-1/255 in a gamma-2 space.
Still (time.w = 1): time 20 s (Nero 35, Verde 50), pointer at the rest position; a still has presence 0, so the sheen sits at rest. Complete as a single frame.
Cost per pixel: fbm2 3 octaves x (4 hash2 + lerp + octave step, ~80 ALU) = ~240, twice = ~480; fbm 4 octaves x ~58 = ~230; veins and halo 5 exp ~60; one palette branch (10 OKLCH colors, 20 sin/cos) ~70; mixes, sheen (2 exp), OKLab to linear, dither ~110. Total ~950 ALU per pixel, no textures, fixed loops of 3 and 4.
Budget: scale 0.5, fps 15 (the motion is a few points a second, so 15 fps is smooth). A 1440 x 900 point window on a 2x screen renders 1440 x 900 = 1.3 M pixels, ~1.23 G ops a frame, estimated ~0.5 ms on an M1-class GPU (~7.5 ms of GPU per second at 15 fps); not measured on a Mac yet. Scale 0.5 keeps the hairlines (about 3 points) crisp; at 0.375 it would be ~0.28 ms with softer hairlines.
Params (6): palette (0 Calacatta, 1 Nero, 2 Verde), vein scale (per height), drift, vein contrast, sheen, warp strength. idle_after 20: it has its own slow motion.
