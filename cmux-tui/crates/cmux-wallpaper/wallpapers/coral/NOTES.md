Coral: point of view, growth. The look of Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion coral and labyrinths without a simulation (the contract forbids feedback buffers). A Turing pattern is, to first order, a random superposition of modes at one critical wavenumber, so the field is that: 12 plane waves of wavelength `wavelength` in evenly spread, slightly hashed directions with hashed phases, normalised to RMS 1, domain-warped by two slow 2-octave value noises (8 wavelengths per noise unit, up to 2 wavelengths deep at `warp` 1) so stripes bend, branch and end like coral instead of reading as a 12-fold quasicrystal.
Growth: thresholding the field at 0 gives the labyrinth, high gives isolated spots. The threshold comes from a growth wave: sin(2 pi (t / cycle - 0.9 rho)) with rho the distance from a seeded origin in 600 point units, roughened by noise, so each place cycles every `growth` minutes from sparse spots through budding worms to a full labyrinth and back, and the front crosses the window at about 1 point a second. The wave phases also creep (up to 0.04 rad/s, about 0.1 point a second), so stripes reconnect slowly. The pointer feeds growth under it (160 point radius, eased with presence).
Shading: AA from the field's analytic gradient (the edge smoothstep is one pixel of field change wide); a rim deeper and more saturated than the core, the core lighter in light themes (translucent tissue) and glowing in dark ones; a faint emboss from the gradient direction lit from the upper left; a soft halo scattered just outside each stripe (`halo`). Paper tinted toward the accent's hue in OKLab; static paper tooth; static triangular +-1 LSB dither.
Variants: Reef (coral on sand), Bone (near-monochrome ivory), Bioluminescent (dark only, glowing teal).
Still: time 150 s, pointer at (0.6, 0.45).
Cost per pixel: warp 4 value noises (16 hashes) ~150 ALU; 12 waves x (2 hashes, 4 sin/cos, ~10 arithmetic) ~540 ALU; growth noise, sin and 1 exp ~50; shading ~40; color (3 OKLab conversions, back to RGB, dither) ~150: ~950 ALU per pixel, one loop of 12, no textures.
Budget: scale 0.5 (1.3 M pixels for a 1440 x 900 point window on a 2x screen; the field is soft and band-limited, so 0.5 loses nothing) at 15 fps: ~1.2 G ops a frame, estimated ~0.5 ms on an M1-class GPU; not measured on a Mac yet. If it measures over, 8 waves (CO_WAVES) keep the look at 2/3 the cost.
Params (8): wavelength (points), contrast, accent (coral toward accent), growth (cycle, minutes), warp, speed, tint (paper), halo.
