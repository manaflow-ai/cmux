# Scene sample

The scene engine's test sample (plans/cmux-next/motion-scenes.md), embedded but not shipped, like
`smoke`. It draws every shape kind once over a four-point mesh gradient in the Behance proteomics
study's palette (lavender, peach, orange, blue): a circle, a half disc, a lens, an eye, four stacked
arcs, a turning round rect, a glass cube lit from the upper right, and a ring. Two blueprint guides
(one solid, one dashed) end in square handles. The field's orange point drifts and the round rect
rocks over the 12 s loop; the light follows the pointer by 30%; the grain is still.

The gallery renders it from Rust's packed block (`examples/scene_frames.rs`), so its stills are the
engine's output for exactly what the app would hand it.
