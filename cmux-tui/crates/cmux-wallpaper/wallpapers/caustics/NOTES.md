Pool: the floor of a pool under light focused by a rippling surface. Point of view: the pool at noon.
What it draws: the caustic network is two layers of cellular seams (Worley F2 - F1 from a 3 x 3 search; each feature point circles slowly at its own rate and phase), the second at 1.55x the scale and rotated ~37 degrees, both seen through one smooth surface swell (nested sines), so the seams curve and the nets interlace. Light = (I1 + 0.7 I2 + 1.2 I1 I2) / 2.9, so crossings are brightest, as in a real pool, times a large slow dapple. Each color channel gets its own line width (red 30% wider, blue 25% narrower at fringe 1), so every bright seam carries a soft warm-to-cool fringe at the cost of one seam evaluation per layer, not three.
Floor: seen through the water with 40% of the light's refraction. Noon and Night Pool: square tiles 7 to a height, each a touch different (+-0.006 L), soft grout. Shallows: sand with soft wind ripples and a fixed fine grain (hash of the pixel, no time term). Lighting is done in linear light as physics has it: albedo x (ambient + gain x caustic light x light tint), so a dark floor stays dark and the caustics are its brightest part. Night Pool has one lamp low on the left wall: the caustics are strongest near it, with a faint teal glow.
Pointer: when the pointer comes to rest (presence.y counts up), a ring spreads from it at 0.045 heights a second, fading in over 0.5 s and out over ~5 s; it bends the light radially as it passes and adds a faint glint. While the pointer moves, presence.y stays near 0 and the ring has no amplitude, so nothing chases the hand.
Motion: the feature points move about 0.024 heights a second at speed 0.6 (~0.015 widths/s at 16:10), the swell slower; inside the 0.05 widths/s envelope. Many local directions, no coherent flow.
Light and dark (presence.w): each pool has a light and dark palette (OKLCH, converted per pixel). Light: floor L 0.80-0.88, caustic peaks to about L 0.96 (ambient 0.9, gain 0.5). Dark: floor L 0.19-0.27, typical seams about L 0.30, crossings up to ~0.38 (ambient 0.7-0.78, gain 1.6, Night Pool 2.4 near the lamp). Triangular dither +-1/255 in a gamma-2 space.
Still (time.w = 1): time 12 s (Night Pool 27, Shallows 41); no ring in a still. Complete as a single frame.
Cost per pixel: one seam evaluation is 9 cells x (hash2, 2 sin, distance and compare, ~34 ALU) = ~306, two layers = ~610; swell 4 sin, ring (length, exp, sin) ~50; 6 exp for the per-channel lines and the dapple ~40; floor (tiles or sand noise) ~30; one palette branch (4 OKLCH colors) ~30; OKLab to linear, lighting, dither ~60. Total ~820 ALU per pixel, no textures, fixed 3 x 3 loops.
Budget: scale 0.5, fps 20. A 1440 x 900 point window on a 2x screen renders 1.3 M pixels, ~1.07 G ops a frame, estimated ~0.42 ms on an M1-class GPU (~8.5 ms of GPU per second at 20 fps); not measured on a Mac yet. Scale 0.375 would be ~0.24 ms with slightly softer seams.
Params (6): palette (0 Noon, 1 Night Pool, 2 Shallows), caustic scale, speed, light strength, chromatic fringe, ring ripple. idle_after 20: it has its own slow motion.
