Fireflies: POV: the window is alive when your agents are. Up to 24 soft motes (`count`, default 16) in one fixed loop (a uniform early break), over a dusky field: the theme's paper a little deeper at the top, with a faint low mist (2 octaves of value noise drifting ~0.002 widths/s) leaning toward the accent.
Each mote: a home on an R2 low-discrepancy sequence (even cover, mostly the lower two thirds), a Lissajous path with a second harmonic at 0.02..0.07 rad/s (under ~0.012 widths/s at speed 1; incommensurate, so the swarm never visibly repeats), a depth (nearer is up to 2x larger, softer and half as bright, like bokeh), and a glow cycle of 5..11 s (0.09..0.2 Hz), eased so it rests lit and rests dark: never a blink. Light per mote: a Lorentzian-squared core plus a Lorentzian halo at 6 radii, one division each and no exp; the halos sum into a soft light in the air around the swarm (the analytic bloom). Two hue ends (accent turned -/+ `spread` in OKLab), four light sums; in dark the light adds (hot cores paler), in light a mote is a deeper tint of the accent on the paper.
Agents (`agents`: true): Frame.activity.x (eased by the host) times `response` brightens the motes (up to 1.2x), keeps more of them lit (pulse floor 0.75) and gathers them up to 30% toward a hearth low in the window, while the mist warms a little. All of it follows the eased level, so nothing jumps on an event. A still uses activity 0.45.
Pointer: the motes follow a point that lags the pointer by about half a second: the trail weighted by sin^2(pi * age / 0.75 s) (a point enters at the head with zero weight and has none left by 0.75 s, before a full ring drops it at ~0.8 s, so nothing jumps) plus the pointer at a small fixed weight, which is all that is left once it rests. A mote within ~0.3 widths leans up to `attract` toward a slow circle (28..88 pt) around that point, so nearby motes drift after you and circle where you rest. The pointer block is skipped by a uniform branch when attract or presence is 0.
Dither: TPDF +-1 LSB in the encoded space (static).
Cost per pixel: trail weighting ~200 ALU (16 rows, 1 sin each; the same for every pixel); per mote ~100 ALU (1 hash42, 5 sin, 2 divisions) or ~130 with the pointer pull (1 exp, 1 sin/cos): ~2000 ALU at 16 motes; mist ~70; colors and dither ~200: ~2300 ALU per pixel.
That is why it renders at scale 0.25 (a soft field: the cores are 1.6 rendered px, upsampled): a 1440 x 900 pt window on a 2x screen is 0.32 M pixels, ~0.75 G ops a frame, estimated ~0.5 ms on an M1-class GPU at 20 fps; 24 motes is ~1.1 G ops (~0.8 ms). At scale 0.5 it would be ~3 G ops (~2 ms), so it is not. idle_after 20. Not measured on a Mac yet.
Params (8): count (6..24), core radius (pt), glow, speed, drawn to the pointer (0..0.8), agent response, mist, hue spread (degrees).
