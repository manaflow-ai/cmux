Truchet: point of view, a pattern that rearranges while you aren't looking. Quarter-circle Truchet tiles (arcs about two opposite corners, so arcs meet edge midpoints and join across tiles into continuous rivers), each river drawn as two thin banks (`river` half-width, `line` points) with a faint water fill between them.
Turning, with no state: every tile has a base orientation (hash), a phase (a smooth value noise over the grid at 0.09 per tile plus 0.06 of hash) and a period P = 6000 / `turns` seconds (turns per minute per 100 tiles). Its orientation at time t is floor(t / P + phase) quarter turns; the newest turn eases (quintic) through its first 3.6 s. Since neighbours' phases are close, turns arrive as a slow front that crosses the field, a few tiles at a time; a 1440 x 900 point window at 34 point tiles (about 1100 tiles) at 3 turns sees about one turn every 2 s, 1 to 2 tiles in motion at once, and each tile turns about every 33 minutes. A turning tile rotates in its slot: its corners clip at the cell edge and the slot it leaves shows a faint shadow; its banks warm toward the accent for the turn (sin of progress, 0.6 deep), so a turn is found when you look, not announced.
Glow: two octaves of value noise at 7 tiles drifting about 1 point a second, gated by a smoothstep, light the water and lean the banks to the accent; the pointer adds a soft pool (3.5 tiles) eased with presence.
Light and dark: OKLab mixing from the theme's paper toward the ink (and accent); a static paper tooth; a static triangular +-1 LSB dither on the final color.
Still: time 120 s, pointer at (0.6, 0.45).
Cost per pixel: tile hashes and phase noise ~70 ALU; one rotation (sin, cos), two arc distances (2 sqrt), bank and water AA ~40; glow noise 8 hashes ~90; pool 1 exp; color (3 OKLab conversions, back to RGB, dither) ~150: ~380 ALU per pixel, no loops, no textures, no neighbour lookups (arcs end perpendicular to the tile edge, so each tile draws only its own).
Budget: scale 0.75 (a 1440 x 900 point window on a 2x screen is 2.9 M pixels) at 20 fps: ~1.1 G ops a frame, estimated ~0.45 ms on an M1-class GPU; not measured on a Mac yet. Scale 0.75 rather than 0.5 because the banks are hairlines on curves: at 0.5 their bilinear upscale smears the arcs into a soft band; if measurement puts it over 0.5 ms, scale 0.5 with `line` 1.0 is the fallback.
Params (8): tile (points), line (points), river (half-width, tile fraction; 0 draws single lines), contrast, turns (per minute per 100 tiles), glow, accent (line toward accent), fill (water).
