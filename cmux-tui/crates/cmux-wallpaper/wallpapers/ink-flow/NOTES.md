Ink: drops of ink blooming in still water. Point of view: slow liquid is the most hypnotic thing on a screen.
What it draws: three drops alive at once, born a third of a lifetime (54 s at speed 1) apart at hashed places inside the middle 72% of the window, so a new one blooms about every 18 s. Each drop is a lobed soft body with a darker rim and pigment streaks; it blooms over ~5 s (9% of its life, no one-frame onset), spreads a little, and fades over the second half of its life.
Flow, analytic: the water's velocity is the curl of a stream function (one octave of gradient noise with its analytic gradient, eddies about a third of the height across, plus two small shear waves at incommensurate angles), so it is divergence-free and a blob stretches into filaments instead of smearing or pooling. There is no feedback buffer: each pixel walks the flow backwards in 6 Euler steps and reads each drop's pigment where its water was at the drop's birth. The field changes very slowly (tau = t/50), so the three drops share one back-path and each picks its own age along it. The pigment streak noise is evaluated at that birth position, so it rides with the water. Peak water speed is about 0.015 heights/s (about 0.009 widths/s at 16:10), typical 0.006, inside the 0.05 widths/s envelope, with many local directions and no coherent full-field flow.
Light and dark (presence.w): the base is the theme's paper in OKLab (L clamped to 0.14..0.97) with a small per-palette tint and fixed washi fibers (value noise stretched 160 x 48 per height, no time term, so it never shimmers). Pigment opacity is 1 - exp(-ink * density * 1.6), capped at 0.85, mixed in OKLab from a thin wash to a dense core. Light: sumi wash to a core at L 0.46 (never black behind text). Dark: luminous pigment, thin L 0.29 to a dense L 0.40-0.46, so the field stays in the dark band. Triangular dither of about +-1/255 in a gamma-2 space, fixed per pixel.
Pointer (stir): near the pointer (Gaussian, ~0.13 heights) the lookup is dragged along the host's smoothed pointer velocity and turned up to ~0.3 rad, scaled by presence.x and eased out over ~2 s after the pointer rests (presence.y), so the ink there swirls with the motion and settles back. A still has no stir.
Still (time.w = 1): time 31 s (variants 23 and 44 s) shows drops at different ages: one young bloom, one unfurled, one fading. Complete as a single frame.
Variants: Sumi (neutral ink, warm washi), Indigo (deep blue, cool paper), Vermilion (red-orange, warm paper; ember in dark).
Cost per pixel: flow step: gradient noise with gradient (4 hash2, ~100 ALU) + two shear waves (2 cos, ~25 ALU) + 3 selects/mixes (~20 ALU) = ~145 ALU, times 6 steps = ~870 ALU. Three drops: atan2, 2 sin, sqrt, 2 exp and one value noise each, ~110 ALU = ~330. Paper: OKLab of the paper (3 pow) and one value noise, ~90. Palette (up to 12 sin/cos), OKLab mix and conversion, dither: ~110. Total ~1400 ALU per pixel, no textures, one fixed loop of 6.
Budget: scale 0.375, fps 20. A 1440 x 900 point window on a 2x screen renders 1080 x 675 = 0.73 M pixels, ~1.0 G ops a frame, estimated ~0.4 ms on an M1-class GPU (about 8 ms of GPU per second at 20 fps); not measured on a Mac yet. If it measures over 0.5 ms, drop IK_STEPS to 5 (-15%) before dropping the scale.
Params (6): palette (0 Sumi, 1 Indigo, 2 Vermilion), flow speed (time multiplier), ink density, eddy scale (stream-function frequency per height), stir, paper texture. idle_after 20: it has its own slow motion.
