title: Attempt Update shows what it is doing
category: fixed

Attempt Update in the command palette now shows its progress and result in the sidebar's update card, like Check for Updates: "Checking", the download's progress, then the ready update with Restart to Update. When you are up to date, the card says so. When the check or the download fails, the card shows the failure with Retry and its details. Before, the action ran with no visible result.
