title: Capture screenshots and recordings straight into a chat
category: new
docs: https://cmux.com/docs/next/agents

Press ⌃⇧⌘5 (or choose View > Capture…) and a capture bar opens over the composer. Click Region, Window, Pane or cmux to take a screenshot, or Record to film the cmux window with the pointer and clicks. What you capture lands in the chat as an attachment, or goes to the clipboard or a file instead.
