---
title: New terminals can split the screen automatically
category: new
try-it: docs/configuration.md#layoutnewpaneplacement-and-layouttilebrowsers
---

Set Settings > General > Columns > New Terminals and Browsers to Split Automatically, and each new terminal gets its own pane: the largest pane on screen splits along its longer side and the new terminal starts in the focused terminal's folder. Docked columns never split.
