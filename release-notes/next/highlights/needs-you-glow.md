title: Waiting tabs and sidebar rows can glow instead of a badge
category: new
docs: https://cmux.com/docs/next/settings
gallery: pages.settings/needs-you-glow

Turn on Settings > Notifications > Needs You Look > Glow, and a tab or sidebar row that waits for you breathes a soft glow in place of its badge until you look at it. The badge stays the default.
