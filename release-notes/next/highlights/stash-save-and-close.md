title: Save and Close tabs and workspaces for later
category: new
action: stash.show | Show Stashed Tabs

Right-click a browser tab, a workspace, a group or a window's workspaces and choose Save and Close. The tabs, or whole workspaces with their layout and terminals (which start fresh when restored), go to the Stashed Tabs page, and Undo brings them back at once. Show Stashed Tabs or `cmux://stashed` opens the page, where you can search, restore one item or a whole group, delete, and import from OneTab.
