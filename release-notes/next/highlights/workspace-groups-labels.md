title: Workspace groups you can see at a glance
action: groupSelectedWorkspaces | Group selected

Each workspace group now shows its name in a colored label, and its workspaces sit on one band of the same color, so a group never looks like a loose list. A click on the header opens or closes the group at once. The arrow keys stop on group headers: Left closes a group, Right opens it, Return renames it.

Ungroup and Delete Group keep every workspace. An undo toast appears, and Cmd-Z brings the group back with its name, color and place.
