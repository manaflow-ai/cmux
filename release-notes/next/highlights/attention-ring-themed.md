title: The unread ring takes your theme's accent, and agent chats stay calm
category: improved

The ring that marks a pane with an unread notification, and its flash when you open the pane, now use your theme's accent color instead of a fixed blue. A finished agent reply no longer rings or flashes its chat pane. Pick the ring's color, its look (glow, ring or none), how many times it flashes, and which panes ring (terminals, agent chats) in Settings > Notifications > Attention Ring.
