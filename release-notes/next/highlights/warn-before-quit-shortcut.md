title: ⌘Q asks before it quits
category: improved
docs: https://cmux.com/docs/next/settings

A stray ⌘Q no longer quits cmux: it asks "Quit cmux?" first, and pressing ⌘Q again quits. Check "Don't ask again" in that question, or turn off Settings > General > Warn Before Quitting with ⌘Q, to quit at once.
