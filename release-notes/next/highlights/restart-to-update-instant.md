title: Restart to Update reacts at once
category: improved

Restart to Update now answers in the next frame: the button shows Installing and the cmux windows leave the screen at once, while cmux finishes the update. The copy of the previous version that cmux keeps for a rollback is now made when the update finishes downloading, not when you click, so the click does no disk work. Terminals and agents keep running across the restart. With unsaved documents, cmux asks about them first, as before.
