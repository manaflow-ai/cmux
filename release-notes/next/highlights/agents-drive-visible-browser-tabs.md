title: Agents drive the browser tabs you can see
category: improved

Agents that use the browser on the Chromium or WebKit engine now open and drive real tabs in your window, not only a hidden browser. A Chromium session gets a Chromium tab, a tab opens even while Home shows, and background agent tabs take clicks, typing and screenshots without taking your focus. When an agent closes a tab it opened, the tab closes. Agents also see the popups their pages open, bring a tab to the front, and use a clipboard of their own that never touches yours.
