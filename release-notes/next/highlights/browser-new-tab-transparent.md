title: The browser's New Tab page shows your window background
category: improved

A new browser tab no longer shows a solid dark page before you go somewhere. In Chromium and WebKit tabs, the New Tab page is transparent, so the window's background and glass show through. Web pages that do not set a background of their own stay white, as in Chrome and Safari.

When you close a tab that is still on the New Tab page, the undo toast now says "Closed “New Tab”", not the page's internal address.
