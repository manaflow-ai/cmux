title: Picking another agent's model forks the chat
category: improved
docs: https://cmux.com/docs/next/agents
gallery: agent-pane.model-switch-fork/forked

Pick another agent's model in a started chat and send: the chat forks onto that agent in its own tab, under a "Forked from" line with the conversation above it. The new agent gets the conversation as its briefing.
