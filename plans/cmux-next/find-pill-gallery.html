Find pill: one design for every Cmd-F

One native Swift pill (FindPillView in CmuxNextDesign) for terminal and browser, and a pixel-matched web twin (FindPill.tsx + find-pill.css) for Agent chat and the diff viewer. Liquid Glass on macOS 26, frosted material on macOS 14–15, solid under Reduce Transparency, no motion under Reduce Motion or the no-animation setting. Each card shows light and dark backdrops. The highlighted card is the recommended one and is the one being built.
