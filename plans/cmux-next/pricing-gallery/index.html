F · Out of credits: inline card in the Chief chat

Recommended in-app design

When the model gateway returns credits_exhausted, Chief posts one card (once per conversation, as today) instead of a plain link. Three ways forward, each one key: top up (opens the signed checkout link), switch this chat to open models, or hand the turn to your own Claude Code on this Mac. Free users see "Upgrade to Pro" instead of top up.

G · Sandbox cost hint when Chief starts one

In-app design

Chief starting a sandbox is the new cost driver, so the floating card that announces it shows size, hourly price and the idle pause. No confirm needed below the spend limit; above it, Approve/Deny with Enter/Esc (same pattern as the pairing card).
