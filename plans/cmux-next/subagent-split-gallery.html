Subagent open: how the split should size

Leo, 2026-11-22: there are also layout shifts galore when i click into a new subagent and it enters a split. and you guys should look at it with a half split, a third split, a quarter split... Each card below plays the same click in all three. The half, third and quarter names follow hq docs/design/split-naming.md: a half, third or quarter split is the side-by-side chain after the open, with 2, 3 or 4 panes, each getting 1/2, 1/3 or 1/4 of the width. In every scene the agent chat is the rightmost pane before the open, and the clicked subagent opens as a new pane right of it. The blue pulse is the click on a subagent row in the chat; the ruler under each stage shows the widths after the open. The outlined card is the one shipped.
