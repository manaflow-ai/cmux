Source: a scratch build that recorded every nested lock acquisition (construction site -> construction site) over the whole-workspace nextest `--profile ci` run: 301 lock classes, 1264 nested pairs, 0 cycles, 89 production holders. Ranks are u16 constants in `lock_rank.rs` (`rank::*`) carried in the lock type, `RankedMutex<T, { rank::X }>`; `lock_rank::Mutex` is the unranked form (not checked). cmux-tui-core uses `lock_rank::{Mutex, MutexGuard, Condvar}` in place of std::sync; three locks that cross into cmux-tui-remote-access `lock_checked` stay `std::sync::Mutex`.
