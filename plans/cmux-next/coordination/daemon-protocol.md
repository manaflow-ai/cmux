- 2026-10-05 (this push) R92 clipboard-read broker, Ghostty config lead: layer 3 (daemon broker), capability `terminal-clipboard-read-v1`. New raw frontend commands `terminal-clipboard-subscribe {terminal_ids}` (at most 256 valid `term_` ids, at most 16 subscribed connections) and `terminal-clipboard-reply {request_id, text?: string|null}` (`{accepted, granted}`; null/absent or over 1 MiB refuses; unknown, answered, cancelled or another connection's request is `accepted:false`), targeted control events `terminal-clipboard-read {request_id (UUID v4), terminal_id, location, host: {kind, name?}}` and `terminal-clipboard-read-cancelled {request_id}`. A hosted surface's host connection signals the broker from its frame reader (no polling) and answers through a weak per-connection replier, never the runtime lock. Refused at once: zero or several live subscribers for the terminal, a terminal with an open read, a frontend holding 16 open reads, a surface without a public terminal id; a newer read from the same surface withdraws the older one; a host cancel withdraws the question; a frontend disconnect refuses its reads. Only client kind `frontend` with derived origin `user` may subscribe or reply, else `origin.forbidden` and no change. No clipboard text is logged. The daemon always sends `host: {kind:"local"}` (its hosts are local to it); a frontend on a remote or Cloud transport names that machine. Not wired: local in-process PTY surfaces (surface/spawn.rs keeps `on_clipboard_read: None`, so their OSC 52 reads stay ignored as before). Layer 2 behavior change: a hosted read nobody can answer is now refused at once instead of staying pending. SDKs regenerated, command count pins 214 -> 216, event pins 58 -> 60. check-spec-inventory.py also scans server/url_open.rs and server/clipboard_read.rs for control events. No Cargo.lock change.
