## Rules
- FREESTYLE-NAMES (a9, Cloud owner, 2026-10-04): Freestyle resource names are `cmuxnp-<env>-<lane>-<rest>`. Team VMs: development `cmuxnp-dev-tvm-`, staging `cmuxnp-stg-tvm-` for every NEW VM from this change on (the driver refuses any other prefix outside production). Existing staging VMs keep their `cmuxnp-dev-tvm-` name until their owner recreates them or the lane's ledger ages them out; TeamVmDO reaches a team's VM only by the id in its record, never by a recomputed name. Deletion only by the lane's ledger, by exact id, never by prefix or by a provider list() call; never delete a user's VM without its owner.
