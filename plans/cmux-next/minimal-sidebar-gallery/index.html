Minimal sidebar — 4 designs

Ask (Lawrence, 2026-10-21): "a minimal custom design for the sidebar that is thinner and takes up virtually 0 horizontal space and is still very clean". Every design is opt-in (sidebar.style); the current sidebar stays the default. In each, pointing at the rail opens the full sidebar over the content (terminals never resize), so Home, groups, drag reorder, the hover card, the search button and Cmd-1…9 hints work as today. Hover a rail below to try it. Left: at rest. Right: open (pointer resting on the rail). With Reduce Motion the sidebar appears in one frame and nothing pulses.

A · Hairline ticks Recommended

6 pt. One short tick per workspace, in sidebar order; groups are small gaps; Home is the round mark on top. The selected workspace's tick is longer and bright, unread is the unread color, working is brighter than idle. Nothing animates at rest. It reads as a tiny minimap: you see where you are, what needs you and what runs, and it costs 6 pt. Ticks stay legible at about 60 workspaces (4 pt each); past what fits, they compress, like a scrollbar.

At rest — 6 pt

Open — full sidebar over the content

B · Dot column

12 pt. A dot per workspace, a ring on the selected one, Home and Search as glyphs at the ends. Calmer than ticks and easier to aim at, but twice the width and the dots crowd past about 40 workspaces.

At rest — 12 pt

C · Mini avatars

24 pt. A 16 pt icon or monogram per workspace with badges, as in Slack or Discord. You can tell workspaces apart without opening the sidebar, but it is a narrow sidebar, not "virtually 0", and 16 pt monograms of agent-named workspaces mostly look the same.

D · Invisible edge

2 pt. Nothing shows except a thin glow on the window edge for unread and working rows. The cleanest, but you cannot see where you are or how many workspaces need you; it is the existing hidden sidebar plus hover. Kept as the floor of the range.

At rest — 2 pt
