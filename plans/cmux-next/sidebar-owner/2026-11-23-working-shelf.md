With `sidebar.workingShelf` on (off by default), an agent chat whose turn ended and that waits on work that wakes it by itself (acpmux's summary `backgroundWaits`: a background task started with notify, a watch to it, a background subagent, a working sub-session) folds into a "Working (N)" row at the end of its list, before the Automatic, Settled, Done and Archived rows; the wait's end starts a turn and the row comes back, as does a question, an unread answer, a failure or a lost agent. Pinned, active, Done and archived rows keep their place. The rule is Rust's (`cmux-layout-reducer` `shelf`, `sidebar_shelf_plan`), cached per content revision (`SidebarModel.shelvedChats`); the row is the existing fold row (`SidebarDoneFoldRowView`, kind `.working`). Off draws every row as before. `SidebarWorkingShelfTests`, gallery `AAStudioWorkingShelfGalleryTests`.
