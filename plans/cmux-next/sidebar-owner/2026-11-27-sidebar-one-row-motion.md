A workspace created or closed is one change of the list: its row goes in or out at its place, the rows under it move by its slot once, no row blanks or is rebuilt, and one row draws the selection. Under lineage nesting (the default since #28103) a row no session started and that started none splices as without it (`SidebarLayout.insertLoose` / `removeLoose`, `splicesUnderTree`); the selection's fold reveal is part of the same render (`SidebarView.render`), and a leaving row drops its selection fill (`clearStraySelection`). No default changes: `sidebar.insertMotion` and `ui.animations.newWorkspace` stay as they were. A sub-session's row drawn above the spliced one takes its new node index too (`renumberEarlierRows`).
