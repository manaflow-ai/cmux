B · Mail-style quote in the text field

The excerpt is inserted as editable "> " text at the top of the field. Simple and fully editable, but it looks like plain text, not a reply, and it is easy to delete by accident.

Like iMessage's reply preview: a small grey pill above my bubble, one line. Cleaner bubble, but the pill floats apart from the reply and reads as metadata.

D · Persistent marker on the original

The quoted words stay underlined in the original, with a reply count (Notes and Docs comments). Great for long Chief reports, heavier for chat; a later step on top of A.
