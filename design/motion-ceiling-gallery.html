Motion ceiling gallery

Every row is a filmstrip: the element drawn at each 120 Hz frame (8.33 ms), from 0 ms until its motion ends, computed from the exact curve. All strips and plots share one time axis, 0 to 300 ms, so end times line up down the page. The green box marks the first frame from which the motion stays within 0.5% of rest; the number under each frame is its progress in percent. A is today, B is the proposal, C is one more option.
