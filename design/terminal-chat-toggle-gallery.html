Toggle Terminal and Chat: the pane control

Leo, 2026-11-12: "we can toggle it, like from the terminal into the GUI, or vice versa, back and forth, right? Like that's the whole joy of the thing." One live session, two faces in one pane: its terminal and a View Only chat. Each variant shows the terminal face and the chat face, dark and light, over a wallpaper through the window's glass. The control is native window chrome, clear on glass, at rest. Spec: design/terminal-chat-toggle.md. HTML mirror of the Swift tokens (TitlebarBandButton, Palette); the shipped control is CmuxNextTabs/PaneTerminalChatToggleView.
