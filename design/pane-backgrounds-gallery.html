Pane backgrounds: four looks

Each look is one combination of the shipped controls: appearance.paneBackgrounds (shared or perPane), pane set-background and pane set-tint. Recommended is the default the pane menu leads with: one shared background, and a marker tint on the panes you want to tell apart (an agent's pane, a production shell).

1. Marker tint Recommended

One shared background. The agent pane gets a 12% purple wash, the prod shell a 12% red wash. Legible, cheap, reads at a glance.
