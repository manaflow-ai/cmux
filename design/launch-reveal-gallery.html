Launch reveal: the cmux mark on the glass, only until the cached first frame

Leo, 2026-11-16: "do we have an initial load logo fade in glass thing like how [a chat app] has theirs/[an issue tracker] has a thing". We did not: the launch window opens on its glass, and each region (sidebar, pane, Home) fades in on its own when its data lands (LaunchReveal); a relaunch whose saved frame is ready draws it in the first frame. Every variant below puts the chevron on that glass in the gap only. Instant-first: the mark waits motion.launch.markDelay (150 ms) after the window's first frame, so a launch whose cached frame is ready sooner never shows it; when the frame is ready the regions fade in (fadeIn, 120 ms) at that same moment, and the mark fades out under them (fadeOut, 80 ms). The mark never holds a region: reveal.* marks land at the same time with or without it (numbers in the PR).

Settled rules kept: a fresh profile's window still opens on the sidebar and the empty main pane, never the mark (cx-r650e, FreshLaunchSidebarTests); the connecting state keeps no mark until a failure (DaemonConnectingView). References: two desktop apps' launches in cmux-design-references (launch-reveal/).

Pick: none recommended yet; Leo picks. Every variant is the launch.reveal Debug Settings choice (off, bloom, lens, trace, sheen) in the PR's build, so each can be tried on a real relaunch. A launch that misses the 1.5 s deadline shows its regions anyway and the mark leaves with them. Spec: design/launch-reveal.md.
