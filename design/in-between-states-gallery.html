In-between states keep the pane's one material

Leo, 2026-11-07: "the loading in-between indicator shouldn't all of a sudden become opaque ... we can just have the small symbols as loading indicators, like really transparent things, if even." Each card is a restoring terminal tab on a glass window over two backdrops. A restoring pane shows its last frame on the glass, never an opaque page; what differs is the indicator. The same rule covers Chromium, which no longer paints an opaque theme color while a page loads: it is see-through until the page paints, as WebKit already was.
