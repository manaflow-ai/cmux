Tab group chips: vibrant glass with a rim

Leo's ask: "Restyle tab group colors as vibrant glass with rim". The chips in a pane's tab strip, the vertical tab list and the saved groups bar, shown on a plain strip and over a wallpaper, dark and light. Each row shows one look at rest, hovered, collapsed with its count, and as a dot (no name). The pill's size is the same in every look: only fill, rim and ink change. A group with no color keeps its clear chip. The look ships in GroupChipGlass (CmuxNextDesign) and follows the sidebar's sidebar.groupGlass and sidebar.groupHeader.background.

1. Vivid glass, role rim Shipped

The sidebar group headers' look (#36225): the color raised in chroma tints the group role's veil at 44% dark, 34% light, with the role's top-lit hairline rim. Follows sidebar.groupGlass, so headers and chips change together.
