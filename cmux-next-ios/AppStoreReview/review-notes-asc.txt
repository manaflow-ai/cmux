cmux for iPhone is where you talk to Chief, an agent that runs your coding agents and reports back in a conversation. You do not need a Mac, a VPN or any network setup to review it: the demo account's Chief runs in cmux Cloud and answers in chat.

Prior review resolutions:
- Guideline 2.5.2 (submission 99c2f5b0-5940-41d4-a02b-fd222d2eeeca): the app downloads and executes no code. Agents run on the user's Mac or in cmux Cloud.
- System VPN API (submission 40f744df-e0fc-4782-9dac-aa0c79281b9f): the binary contains no packet tunnel extension and no Network Extension or VPN entitlement.
- Guideline 1.5: the Support URL is https://cmux.com/support.

Sign in (there is no password):
1. Launch cmux and tap "Continue with email". On "What's your email?", enter {{DEMO_ACCOUNT_EMAIL}} (also in the demo account field) and tap "Continue".
2. The app emails a one-time code. Open this public inbox in Safari: {{MAILINATOR_PUBLIC_INBOX_URL}} (no account or password needed). The newest email holds the code.
3. Type the code on "Check your email". It submits on the last character. If no code arrives within two minutes, tap "Resend code".

First launch:
- If "Choose a space" appears, tap "Personal".
- "Connect your Mac" is for people who also run cmux on a Mac. Tap "Not now".
- On "Know when an agent needs you", tap "Turn on notifications" or "Not now".

## Hosted Chief answers in chat, with no Mac.
Review path (no Mac needed):
1. Home opens. Tap Chief (it is labeled Main · Cloud), type a message, for example: What can you help me with? Then tap Send. Chief answers in the conversation within a few seconds.
2. If Home shows "Chief Is Off" instead, tap "Turn On", then open Chief and send the message.
3. Tap Chief's name at the top. Its page says "This Chief runs in cmux Cloud."
## REVIEW DEMO STEPS: END

Simulated Mac (this account only): to try terminals and browser tabs without a Mac, the demo account has a simulated Mac named MacBook Pro, run on the iPhone. It is not a real computer, and no other account has it.
1. Open the Workspaces tab. It lists the MacBook Pro's three workspaces: website, api-server and notes.
2. Tap website. Its terminal opens and shows recent commands. Type a command such as ls, git status or htop and send it; the terminal answers.
3. Tap Tabs, then the cmux tab. The page opens in the app.

Other screens:
- Home: swipe a conversation left for "Read" and "Pin"; touch and hold it for "Mark as Read". "New Message" (top right): pick a Chief or teammate under "To:" and send.
- For other accounts, the Workspaces and Feed tabs show the workspaces and alerts of a Mac signed in to the same account. With no Mac they offer "Connect your Mac".
- Account deletion: Settings, the account row at the top opens "Account", then "Delete Account". After "Delete your cmux account?" the account and its data are deleted and the app signs out. Please try it last.

Permissions, each optional and asked only when first used:
- Notifications: during setup or the first message to Chief.
- Microphone: calls and audio messages.
- Speech Recognition: voice notes to text, on device.
- Camera: a photo for a message, or a Mac's pairing code.
- Photos: attaching and saving images.
- Face ID: only when "Require Face ID" is on in Settings.
- Local Network: a direct link to the user's own Mac on the same network.

Background audio (UIBackgroundModes audio): an audio message or call with Chief keeps recording while the iPhone is locked. To see it, start an audio message to Chief, lock the iPhone a few seconds, unlock and send: it includes what was said while locked.

In-app purchases:
- Settings > "Plan" opens "Plans": cmux Go, cmux Pro and cmux Max, monthly; "Subscribe" buys in the Sandbox. "Restore Purchases" (and "Manage Subscription" when subscribed), "Terms of Use", "Privacy Policy" and the 3.1.2 terms (renews until cancelled) are there. No other purchase links.
