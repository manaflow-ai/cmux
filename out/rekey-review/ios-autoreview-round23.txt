Packages/iOS/CmuxMobileShell/Sources/CmuxMobileShell/PairedMacRestore.swift:328

A recently refreshed stale copy therefore bypasses suppression; `resolveParkedTombstones` also retires the account-wide intent, allowing the supposedly forgotten Mac to restore instead of receiving a delete.
