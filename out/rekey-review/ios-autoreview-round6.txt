Packages/iOS/CmuxMobileShell/Sources/CmuxMobileShell/BackingUpPairedMacStore.swift:72
The local store explicitly permits the same account/device/tag pairing to exist in multiple team scopes, but this key contains only the account and pairing ID. An upload or restore in team B therefore overwrites team A's destination. Later, `flushPendingDeletes` can route A's tombstone to B and remove the shared mapping, causing A's backup to resurrect or a tag-exact forget to delete the unrelated team-B backup. [P1] Activation freezes credentials for the lifetime of the runtime
ios/cmuxPackage/Sources/cmuxFeature/MobileIrohRuntimeComposition.swift:1391
The broker retained by `CmxIrohClientRuntime` and the scheduled relay-policy service closes over one activation-time snapshot. Automatic relay credential refresh, discovery, and other broker operations then fail until an unrelated lifecycle reconcile rebuilds the runtime.
