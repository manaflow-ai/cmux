Packages/Shared/CmuxAuthRuntime/Sources/CmuxAuthRuntime/Coordinator/AuthCoordinator.swift:614

[P1] [P1] Do not treat a remote re-pair as a stale backup record
Packages/iOS/CmuxMobileShell/Sources/CmuxMobileShell/BackingUpPairedMacStore.swift:146
A parked intent matches every later snapshot solely by pairing ID and unconditionally sends another delete. It is cleared only by a local `BackingUpPairedMacStore.upsert`, so if another phone re-pairs/revives this Mac, the forgetting phone never observes that clear: its next restore interprets the newly revived server record as stale, deletes it again, and retains the parked intent indefinitely. This contradicts the UI promise that the Mac reappears after reconnecting and can make cross-device re-pairing impossible to persist. The tombstone needs a server revision/time boundary or another authoritative revival signal so records created after the forget retire the local parked intent.
